## How to review them

For each problem, make sure you can answer:

* What pattern does it use?
* What is the time complexity?
* What is the space complexity?
* Could I explain my solution to an interviewer?
* Could I solve it again without looking at notes?

If you can confidently solve and explain these problems, you'll have covered the patterns that appear in a large percentage of entry-level software engineering interviews.

After you're comfortable with this list, the next step is to add about **20–30 carefully chosen Medium problems** that are commonly asked in interviews to deepen your understanding.
